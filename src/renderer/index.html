Welcome aboard

        
Meet NexusChat Open.

        
One desktop app for chat and scheduled bots — running on your machine, with your own provider key. Free and open source.

        

          
        

        

      

      

        
One more thing

        
Bring your own key.

        
NexusChat Open never sells model access. Paste a provider key in the Providers tab — it is encrypted and stays on this PC.

        

          
          
Tip: local Ollama models need no key at all — install Ollama, pull a model, and chat works with Wi-Fi off.
